## The agents we build

| Agent | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Contract Agent** | Reviews contracts before you sign. It reads the contract in front of you against a standard we built from years of negotiating deals, and your own positions sharpen it over time. |
| **Sales Agent** | Helps your team work deals. It answers what your salespeople ask in the middle of a deal: what you charge, how to answer pushback, where the deal stands. |
| **Data Analyst Agent** | Turns piles of data into clear reports. Your team can ask it questions in plain English, and every number shows where it came from. |
| **Data QA Agent** | Checks your data before it ships, against a quality standard we built from years of shipping people and company data. You find the problems before your customers do. |

Every agent says where each answer came from, asks instead of guessing when it is
not sure, keeps the corrections your team makes, and leaves a record of everything
it did that you can check yourself.

## How it works

1. **We learn how you work.** A business expert from our team spends the first
   few weeks with yours, learning how the work gets done, where the time goes,
   and where you need help.
2. **We pick the work.** Together we pick the workflows worth giving to agents,
   and we quote a price for each one before we build it.
3. **We build and launch.** The agents come with our know-how built in, tailored
   to your company, and go live in the Slack your team already uses by a date we
   set with you.
4. **We stay on.** We keep improving them after launch. When your team corrects
   an agent, the correction sticks.

You pay for the work, not for seats. Your knowledge stays yours: everything we
write down about your company, and everything your agents write, belongs to you,
and you can export all of it whenever you want.
